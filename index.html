Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. Open it and start writing — there’s nothing to sign up for.

Where exactly are my notes?

In your browser’s own storage on this device. They aren’t sent anywhere unless you share a note as a link or export it yourself.

Can I use it on my phone and laptop?

Yes, and you can install it like an app on both. Each browser keeps its own notes. When you want them to match, open Sync my devices on both and scan a code — the notes go straight from one to the other over your Wi-Fi, never through a server.

What happens if I clear my browser?

Your notes are cleared too. That’s why Folio56 nudges you to back up, and can save a copy to a folder every day.

Does it use AI?

Only if you switch it on and add your own free key. Until then, nothing you write goes to any AI.
